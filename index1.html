The Challenge

Bridging the Gap Between Science and System

In Nepal, pharmacy education excels at teaching clinical and industrial sciences. However, there is a massive gap when it comes to understanding how health policies are made, how federalism impacts healthcare, and how pharmacists can advocate for change.

The Pharmacy in Health Policy Symposium is a premier, one-day intensive event designed by CPREC Nepal. We are bringing together students, early-career pharmacists, regulators, and public health experts to transform today's students into tomorrow's healthcare leaders.

Understand the System: Learn how MoHP and DDA function in federal Nepal.
Learn to Advocate: Master communication on issues like AMR.
Hands-on Skills: Write actual, data-driven Policy Briefs.
Direct Networking: Connect with experts shaping National Drug Policy.

Key Objectives

Promote Youth Leadership

Empower students to move into policymaking roles and drive healthcare reform.

Enhance Advocacy Skills

Educate on antibiotic resistance, medication safety, and public health communication.

Build Research Capacity

Training in methodology, data analysis, and evidence-based policy formulation.

Program

The 4-Stage Experience

Click each stage to explore what you'll learn and experience throughout the symposium.

Stage I

Foundations

Health Policy 101

An introduction to evidence-based policymaking in Nepal.

  • Federalism & Pharmacy: Navigating local, provincial, and federal health structures
  • The Policy Lifecycle: How a health crisis turns into a national law
Stage II

Advocacy

The Pharmacist's Voice

Strategies for high-impact public health advocacy.

  • AMR & One Health: Taking a stand on antibiotic resistance
  • Strategic Communication: How to speak so bureaucrats and the public listen
Stage III

Workshop

Research & Data (Hands-on)

Turning raw data into actionable policy.

  • Open Health Data: Utilizing Nepal's HMIS for pharmacy research
  • The Policy Brief: Guided group activity to draft a 1-page brief
Stage IV

Dialogue

Interactive Q&A Panel

Open, unfiltered discussions with the experts.

  • The Future of Pharmacy Practice: Bridging the gap between law and reality
  • Rapid-Fire Q&A: Ask your toughest questions directly

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Will I get a certificate?

Yes, all attendees who complete the full-day program and the Stage III workshop will receive a certified physical Certificate of Participation from CPREC Nepal.

Do I need to bring a laptop?

No, a laptop is not mandatory. However, please bring a smartphone for digital surveys. We will provide all necessary stationery (notebooks, pens, chart papers) for the workshop.

Can non-pharmacy students attend?

While the core focus is on pharmacy practice, students of Public Health (BPH), Nursing, and Medicine who are passionate about national health policy are welcome to apply.

What is included in the registration fee?

The registration fee includes full-day access to all sessions, a comprehensive Symposium Kit, Snacks, 2 tea/coffee breaks, and a verified Certificate of Participation.
